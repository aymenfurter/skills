# Sample sources and asset notices

## AI Engineer Coach

`ai-engineer-coach-gpt-6-astra.png` and `ai-engineer-coach-gpt-5.6-sol.png` show
[microsoft/AI-Engineering-Coach at revision 18b1a3d](https://github.com/microsoft/AI-Engineering-Coach/tree/18b1a3d16b586c171426c6a407cc5c2dc073556e).
The initial proposal used `drawio-pitch`. GPT-6 Astra and GPT-5.6 Sol each
refined that same proposal through four `drawio-iterate` passes. These are A5
architecture illustrations, not complete call graphs or runtime validation.
The Sol image is retained as a reference, including its visible label clipping
and omitted secondary connectors.

The visual direction follows the project's
[dashboard](https://github.com/microsoft/AI-Engineering-Coach/blob/18b1a3d16b586c171426c6a407cc5c2dc073556e/assets/screen-dashboard.png)
and [theme](https://github.com/microsoft/AI-Engineering-Coach/blob/18b1a3d16b586c171426c6a407cc5c2dc073556e/src/webview/styles.css).
The screenshot itself is not included.
